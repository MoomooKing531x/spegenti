How to make a pasta

This is a recipe for Pasta I chose this recipe because I like eating pasta and I often cook it because it is an easy and delicious delicacy. I eat it often when I am hungry, and adding meat makes it more yummy and fulfilling. The original recipe I used can be found here.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef or turkey
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 jar (approx. 25 oz.) marinara sauce
  • 12 ounces pasta (such as rotini, rigatoni, or farfalle)
  • Heat water to 100°C & prepare ingredients before cooking.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat, add the ground beef, and cook until browned, breaking it into small pieces.
  2. Reduce the heat to medium, then stir in the marinara sauce, water, and salt.
  3. Add the pasta to the pot, stir everything together, and simmer for about 15 minutes while stirring frequently.
  4. Pour in an extra cup of water, continue simmering for another 15 minutes until the pasta is tender, and serve immediately.
